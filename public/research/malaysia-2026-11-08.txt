Method and interpretation
Capture limits and request budgets are recorded in the manifest. Collection position is not assumed to be sales rank. Catalogue presence, search interest, creator discussion and verified publication metadata are distinct signals.

Title matching uses exact normalised phrases and remains reviewable. TF-IDF similarity measures metadata relevance, not reader demand. Keyword rules label anticipation, recommendations and enquiries without claiming sentiment-model calibration. Geographic labels distinguish an explicit place mention from a Malaysia-targeted search.

Trends series are normalised per request. The latest bucket is excluded from momentum features. A minimum of 12 prior buckets and eight nonzero buckets within that window is required. Momentum compares the recent four-bucket mean with the previous eight. City-level interest is shown only if provided by the source.

Saved retailer snapshots support comparison after a second successful capture day. Failed sources do not become zero demand. A newly captured title is not automatically a new release. Backlist revival and stockout forecasting require verified publication history and forward validation.

Raw authenticated response metadata and credentials are excluded from exports. Public snippets and links should be reviewed under source access, retention and reuse rules before recurring commercial distribution.
